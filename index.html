Engineer Hub

Plumbing & HVAC at your fingertips

Choose the system, manufacturer and exact model. Manuals, fault help, calculators, reports and saved PDFs stay together.

Engineer use: Always use the exact current manufacturer instructions for the appliance, fuel/refrigerant and model revision. Gas, LPG, oil, heat-pump and refrigerant work must be carried out only within the engineer's competence and applicable registration/certification.

⚠️ Fault Code Assistant

Quick diagnosis
Choose the manufacturer, optionally choose the exact model, then enter the displayed fault code. Spaces, dots, slashes and letter case are handled automatically. PlumbCore only gives a local answer where the code has been verified. Every manufacturer/model in the library can still be routed to its official manuals and troubleshooting search, so an unknown code is never guessed.
Coverage: Boiler, LPG, oil, heat-pump and Air-Con / F-Gas manufacturers in the catalogue are searchable. Local quick-code meanings are deliberately limited to entries verified against manufacturer literature; all other codes route to exact-model official sources.

Launch & Data Safety

PlumbCore launch check

Run this before relying on PlumbCore for live customer work. It checks browser storage, backup status, technical-library integrity and core release metadata.

Data & privacy: Customer records, jobs, quotes, invoices, settings and reports in this build are stored in browser storage on this device. PlumbCore does not upload those records to a PlumbCore cloud account. Opening manufacturer manuals or payment links leaves the app and connects to the selected third-party website. The business using PlumbCore remains responsible for its customer privacy notice, retention policy and lawful handling of personal data.
Technical & safety: PlumbCore is an engineer aid, not a substitute for current manufacturer instructions, regulations, competent-person requirements, combustion analysis, electrical safety procedures or F-Gas obligations. Exact model/revision must be confirmed before acting on technical information.

Launch Documents

Public release documents: Keep the product privacy notice, terms and support information available wherever PlumbCore is supplied. These documents describe the local-storage design, third-party manufacturer links, backup responsibilities and technical-safety limits.
For a paid public release, add the seller/operator's legal name, postal contact and dedicated support contact to the public listing and these documents before taking payment.
