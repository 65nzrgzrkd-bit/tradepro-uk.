Product Privacy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Privacy by design: The main customer, job, quote, invoice and settings records are stored in browser storage on the device in this build.

Information stored on your device

This can include business settings, customer contact details, jobs, diary entries, quotes, invoices, price-book changes and engineer reports.

Third-party websites

Opening an official manufacturer manual, map, payment page or other external link leaves FIXTIVO and connects to that provider. Their own privacy policies apply.

Payment information

Where payment is handled by an external payment provider, card details are entered with that provider. FIXTIVO does not need to store full card details in local job records.

Customer data

The business using FIXTIVO is responsible for deciding why and how its customer information is used, keeping it secure and complying with applicable data-protection law.

Backups and deletion

Users should export regular backups. Local operational records can be removed by clearing FIXTIVO site/browser storage on the device. Clearing browser/site data, removing the app, changing devices or using private-browsing modes may also remove local records. Exported backup files are separate and must be deleted separately by the user.

Hosted sign-in and subscriptions

The main job records described above stay on your device. Account access is different: FIXTIVO uses Supabase for email sign-in and subscription-access checks. Hosted subscription records can include your email address, Stripe customer and subscription references, subscription status and billing-period information. These records are used to provide and manage access to the service.

Optional appliance plate scanning

If you choose AI plate scanning, the selected image is sent through FIXTIVO’s Supabase service to OpenAI for analysis. The returned equipment details are shown for you to review. Do not include customer documents, people or unrelated personal information in a plate image. You can enter appliance information manually instead.

Service providers

The public app page is hosted through GitHub Pages. Supabase supports authentication, account-access checks and server functions; Stripe handles subscription checkout; OpenAI processes images submitted to the optional scanner. These services may process connection and usage information when you use them. See GitHub’s privacy statement, Supabase’s privacy policy, Stripe’s privacy policy and OpenAI’s privacy policy for provider information.

Contact and requests

FIXTIVO is operated by G. McNeill. For account-data questions, access, correction or deletion requests, contact plumbcoreuk@outlook.com. Deleting local browser records does not cancel a subscription or delete hosted account and billing records. Request subscription cancellation separately using your checkout email address.

You can also raise a data-protection concern with the Information Commissioner’s Office.
